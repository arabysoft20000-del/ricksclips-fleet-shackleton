# Title, description and tags (only when the user asks)

The output is for publishing the video as a YouTube Short. Write in clear English, in your own words, from SHORT/script.md and SHORT/facts.md. Ask which short if it is not clear. Read the first line of SHORT/facts.md: `Story type: real` or `Story type: made-up`.

## Title (one)
- 4 to 8 words, Title Case. No digits, no emoji, no hashtags, no ALL CAPS words.
- It names the odd object, place or rule and hides the twist. Its key term returns in the script's pivot.
- Choose one of the shapes that fit the story:
  - "The [Thing] That / Who [Did Something Odd]" or "The [Thing] Built Around [Thing]"
  - "Why [Subject] [Does Something Surprising]"
  - "This [Person or Thing] [Did Something Odd]"
  - "How [Subject] [Did Something] [Event]"
  - a direct question: "What If...?" or "Would You...?"
  - a plain statement naming the odd thing
- Verbs of surprise help (rigged, saved, broke, warned, hates). Never promise anything the video does not show; the word free never appears. For a made-up story, never present it as a real event.

## Description
2 to 3 plain lines: what happens in the story and what the viewer will understand, without the twist. No promises of views, no "like and subscribe". For a made-up story only, end with one short line: "This story is fictional." A real story gets no disclaimer.

## Tags
5 to 7 short, direct tags: the topic, the place or era, the field, and the key object or person. No unrelated trending words.

## Layout of the answer
```
Title
...

Description
...

Tags
tag one, tag two, ...
```
Save the same text to SHORT/seo.md.
